# CW-10 Five-Dimension Planetary Reasoning

## Scope and implementation

The generic D1 engine trace keeps intrinsic strength, structural relationships,
environmental influence, and input completeness as separate fields. Aspect and
co-location records retain their source provenance; the generic layer does not
assign a condition verdict to a conjunction. Career adapts that trace to its
existing C6 inputs and records unavailable career affliction as an explicit
missing factor. The existing C6 and C9 policies remain authoritative.

The engine matrix uses synthetic charts for dignity, combustion, motion,
benefic/malefic/mixed/multiple/no aspects, conjunction provenance, structural
relationships, and incomplete inputs. It also checks cross-dimension isolation,
two ascendant-dependent functional classifications, and the live chart as one
fixture. The Cartesian portion covers 2,592 combinations. Missing inputs remain
distinguishable from resolved-empty inputs.

## CW-10G–10J integrity follow-up

Aspect records now identify the natural rule and functional source used for
classification. Malformed canonical records are skipped with diagnostics and
make aspect availability `PARTIAL`; a resolved empty aspect list remains
`AVAILABLE`. Career C6 consumes only directionally classified natural-benefic
and natural-malefic buckets. Mixed, unassessed, and functional-only records are
retained as non-consumed provenance and cannot create C6 support or pressure.

Trace completeness is reported for the stable factors `dignity`, `combustion`,
`motion`, `shadbala`, `ownedHouses`, `occupiedHouse`, `aspectSources`,
`coLocatedPlanets`, `relationships`, and `yogas`. Resolved-empty relationship
inputs remain available. Complete Shadbala totals must be finite and
non-negative. Relationship participant data is validated and deduplicated;
exchanges are marked `OBSERVED` unless independent ownership and placement
facts confirm them. Co-location uses position-level house/sign together, or
fact-level house/sign only when the position object is absent. Angular
separation is geometric provenance, not an effect classification.

Each trace carries only calculation methodology and engine version metadata;
it contains no birth details. The end-to-end regression matrix exercises three
ascendants and all ten natural-by-functional classification combinations
through trace, C6, C8, and C9. These integrity changes do not alter C6 verdict
policy or C9 predicates.

## CW-10F policy review

The matrix validates provenance isolation and completeness reporting; it does
not provide outcome evidence for changing C6 thresholds. Therefore:

- A lone malefic aspect continues to contribute the existing C6 malefic-pressure
  factor and can resolve to `WEAK` when other condition inputs are neutral and
  available. The career adapter test locks this behavior.
- Co-location is recorded as provenance only. It does not contribute a new C6
  condition factor. The matrix does not justify assigning conjunction weight
  or a condition effect.
- C9 continues to map a `WEAK` condition to `CHALLENGE` only under its existing
  relevance/expression predicates; the existing C9 activation tests remain the
  policy contract.
- Missing combustion, aspects, or other inputs remain explicitly incomplete;
  they do not produce negative evidence.

No C6 threshold or C9 verdict predicate was changed. A policy change would
require outcome-level validation beyond this provenance and isolation matrix.
