## Purpose

P2-05 will freeze the advanced Yoga model for Career intelligence, defining how Yoga patterns are detected, classified, and qualified. This is explicitly a later P2 document (not in initial scope). Yoga is treated as Potential Pattern, not prediction.

## Outline (to be frozen when activated)

- Yoga as Potential Pattern
  - Yoga represents potential, not prediction
  - Yoga mechanism explains HOW, not WHAT will happen
  - Yoga activation depends on Dasha, condition, career relevance

- Yoga graph object definition
  - participants (planets involved)
  - house relationships (which houses are involved)
  - lordships (which lords participate)
  - strength (yoga strength, not prediction score)
  - condition (planetary conditions of participants)
  - career relevance (connection to career houses/lords)
  - Dasha participants (which Dasha lords activate this yoga)
  - D10 confirmation (does yoga manifest in D10?)

- Yoga classification fields
  - Classical yoga categories (Raja Yoga, Dharma Yoga, Artha Yoga, etc.)
  - Career-specific yoga classifications (RAJA_YOGA_CAREER, DHARMA_KARMA_ALIGNMENT, etc.)
  - House-based yoga (kendra-trikona, trikona-trikona, etc.)
  - Planet-based yoga (Sun-Moon, Jupiter-Venus, etc.)

- Initial yoga families (frozen in P2-00 Family B)
  - RAJA_YOGA_CAREER (kendra-trikona lord combination)
  - DHARMA_KARMA_ALIGNMENT (1/5/9 lord with 10H involvement)
  - AUTHORITY_PATTERN (kendra lord strength)
  - PROFESSIONAL_RISE_PATTERN (specific planet combinations)

- Yoga detection rules
  - Lord co-presence (lords in same house)
  - Lord aspect (lords aspecting each other)
  - Lord exchange (parivartana)
  - House involvement (kendra + trikona)
  - Condition verification (dignity, strength, affliction)

- Yoga qualification
  - Structural Strength (how strong is the yoga foundation?)
  - Planetary Condition (how well-conditioned are participants?)
  - Career Relevance (how connected to career?)
  - Pattern Coherence (how coherent are relationships?)
  - Activation Potential (Dasha participants)
  - Divisional Confirmation (D9/D10)

- Yoga mechanism explanation
  - Explains HOW the yoga operates (e.g., "kendra-trikona lord exchange creates authority potential")
  - Does NOT predict outcome (e.g., "will become CEO")
  - Route through planetary condition → career-house connection → professional mechanism

- Advanced yoga taxonomy (deferred)
  - Complex Raja Yoga variants
  - Nabhasa yogas
  - Karaka-based yogas
  - Varga-based yogas

- TypeScript interface for Yoga graph (exact shape)
